# Wild India Atlas

**A record of India's extinct and endangered wildlife.**

Wild India Atlas is a website that keeps records of the wild species of India that have been lost or are at risk of being lost. The goal is simple: to give every extinct and endangered species a place on the map, so that people can learn what India has, what it has lost, and what still needs protecting.

> 🔗 **Live site:** [wildindiaatlas.netlify.app](https://wildindiaatlas.netlify.app)

---

## About

India is home to a huge range of wildlife, from Himalayan peaks to the Western Ghats, the Sundarbans and the grasslands of the north. Many of these species are now threatened, and some are already gone. Information about them is scattered across reports, papers and databases.

Wild India Atlas brings that information together in one place, in a clear and readable form.

## What the Atlas covers

- **Extinct species**: animals that once lived in India and no longer exist.
- **Endangered species**: animals that are at serious risk of disappearing.
- **Species records**: each entry is meant to hold the species' name, status, where it lives or lived, and why it is under threat.
